Our Story

Cooking the way our grandmothers did, with better wine.

The Copper Fig started as a Sunday supper club in a two-bedroom apartment. Six years later we have a wood oven, forty seats, and the same rule: if it isn't in season, it isn't on the menu.

Chef Mara Bellini writes the menu every Tuesday after the farmers market run. Expect hand-rolled pasta, whole fish for two, and vegetables treated like the main event.

Mara Bellini, Chef & Owner
